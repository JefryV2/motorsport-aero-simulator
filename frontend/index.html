What the metrics mean

Downforce (N)
Vertical force pressing the car onto the track. More downforce = more grip in corners, but also more drag.
Drag (N)
Resistance from pushing air aside. Lower drag helps straight-line speed and reduces fuel/energy use.
L/D (lift-to-drag ratio)
Downforce divided by drag — how much grip you buy per unit of drag cost. Higher is more efficient.
Balance % front
How much total downforce acts on the front axle. Affects turn-in and understeer/oversteer balance (typical F1 target ~40–45%).
Lap Δ (s)
Estimated lap time change from your current aero setup vs a baseline — useful for comparing wing angles and ride heights.
Cp (pressure on car)
Blue = suction (low pressure), red = stagnation (high pressure at nose/wings).
Air speed colors
Particles and streamlines change color with local speed — warm/orange at slow regions (nose stagnation), blue/white where air accelerates (exit, Venturi). Toggle Surface speed view to see speed on the car body.
Probe tool
Enable Probe in the toolbar, then click the car to read local Cp and velocity at that point.
