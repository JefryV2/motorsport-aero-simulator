# ADR NNNN: Short title of the decision



- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR NNNN

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

- **Phase / task:** e.g. Phase 1, P1.4



## Context



What problem or choice is this about? What constraints apply (compute, time, data, accuracy, existing code)?



## Options considered



1. **Option A** — short description. Pros / cons.

2. **Option B** — short description. Pros / cons.

3. **Option C** (optional)



## Decision



What was chosen, and why this option over the others.



## Consequences



- What becomes easier or harder.

- What must be tested or validated because of this choice.

- What existing code is affected (and whether a golden or regression test covers it).

- Known risks and how they are mitigated.



## Approval



For any change that rewrites or removes a working module, record the human approval here (who, when).
